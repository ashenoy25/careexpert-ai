# Family Caregiver Alliance (FCA) - Resources Catalog

**Source:** Family Caregiver Alliance (FCA) / National Center on Caregiving
**Base URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/caregiver-resources/all-resources/
**Organization:** Family Caregiver Alliance, San Francisco, CA
**Date Compiled:** 2026-02-11
**Note:** FCA is a leading national nonprofit addressing the needs of families and friends providing long-term care for loved ones at home. Founded in 1977, FCA was the first community-based nonprofit in the US to address the needs of families and friends providing long-term care at home.

---

## 1. CONDITION-SPECIFIC FACT SHEETS

### 1.1 Dementia and Alzheimer's Disease

#### Caregiver's Guide to Understanding Dementia Behaviors
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/caregivers-guide-understanding-dementia-behaviors/
- **Topics:** Behavioral symptoms of dementia, communication strategies, wandering, agitation, sundowning, repetitive behaviors, aggression, sleep disturbances
- **Summary:** Comprehensive guide explaining common behavioral changes in dementia, including why they occur and practical strategies for caregivers to manage them. Covers communication techniques, environmental modifications, and when to seek professional help. Addresses the emotional toll on caregivers witnessing personality changes.
- **Key subtopics:**
  - Communication breakdown and strategies
  - Managing agitation and aggression
  - Sundowning and sleep disturbances
  - Wandering prevention and safety
  - Repetitive questions and behaviors
  - Hallucinations and delusions
  - Personal care resistance

#### Dementia, Caregiving, and Controlling Frustration
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/dementia-caregiving-and-controlling-frustration/
- **Topics:** Emotional management, frustration coping, anger in caregiving, communication with dementia patients
- **Summary:** Addresses the frustration and anger caregivers commonly experience when caring for someone with dementia. Provides strategies for managing emotional responses, reframing expectations, and maintaining compassion during challenging moments.

#### Alzheimer's Disease
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/alzheimers-disease/
- **Topics:** Alzheimer's disease overview, stages, symptoms, diagnosis, treatment, caregiving implications
- **Summary:** Fact sheet covering the basics of Alzheimer's disease including risk factors, progression through stages (early, middle, late), current treatments, and practical caregiving considerations at each stage. Includes information about research and clinical trials.
- **Key subtopics:**
  - Disease stages and progression
  - Diagnostic process
  - Medications (cholinesterase inhibitors, memantine, newer anti-amyloid therapies)
  - Planning for the future
  - Care at each stage

#### Frontotemporal Dementia
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/frontotemporal-dementia/
- **Topics:** FTD types, behavioral variant FTD, primary progressive aphasia, diagnosis, management
- **Summary:** Explains the different types of frontotemporal dementia, how they differ from Alzheimer's, early onset considerations, behavioral and language symptoms, and caregiving challenges unique to FTD.

#### Dementia with Lewy Bodies
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/dementia-lewy-bodies/
- **Topics:** Lewy body dementia, visual hallucinations, motor symptoms, fluctuating cognition, medication sensitivity
- **Summary:** Covers the characteristics of Lewy body dementia, the overlap with Parkinson's disease, unique symptoms including visual hallucinations and REM sleep behavior disorder, and critical medication sensitivities (especially to antipsychotics).

#### Vascular Dementia
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/vascular-dementia/
- **Topics:** Vascular dementia, multi-infarct dementia, stroke-related cognitive decline, risk factor management
- **Summary:** Describes vascular dementia caused by reduced blood flow to the brain, its relationship to stroke and cardiovascular disease, stepwise progression pattern, and preventive strategies including management of hypertension, diabetes, and cholesterol.

### 1.2 Stroke

#### Stroke
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/stroke/
- **Topics:** Stroke types, warning signs, recovery, rehabilitation, caregiver role in stroke recovery
- **Summary:** Covers ischemic and hemorrhagic stroke, risk factors, the importance of rapid treatment (FAST acronym), rehabilitation process, and long-term caregiving needs. Addresses physical, cognitive, and emotional effects of stroke.
- **Key subtopics:**
  - Types of stroke (ischemic, hemorrhagic, TIA)
  - Warning signs and emergency response
  - Rehabilitation therapies (physical, occupational, speech)
  - Cognitive and emotional changes after stroke
  - Caregiver role in recovery
  - Secondary stroke prevention

#### Aphasia
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/aphasia/
- **Topics:** Aphasia types, communication strategies, speech therapy, stroke communication recovery
- **Summary:** Explains aphasia (language impairment often resulting from stroke), different types (Broca's, Wernicke's, global), communication strategies for caregivers, and the role of speech-language pathology in recovery.

### 1.3 Parkinson's Disease

#### Parkinson's Disease
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/parkinsons-disease/
- **Topics:** Parkinson's disease overview, motor and non-motor symptoms, medications, caregiving progression
- **Summary:** Comprehensive overview of Parkinson's disease covering motor symptoms (tremor, rigidity, bradykinesia, postural instability), non-motor symptoms (depression, cognitive changes, sleep disorders), medication management (levodopa, dopamine agonists), surgical options (deep brain stimulation), and caregiving considerations as the disease progresses.
- **Key subtopics:**
  - Motor and non-motor symptoms
  - Medication management and timing
  - Fall prevention
  - Cognitive changes and Parkinson's dementia
  - Depression and anxiety in PD
  - Deep brain stimulation (DBS)
  - Late-stage care

### 1.4 Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS)

#### Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS)
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/amyotrophic-lateral-sclerosis/
- **Topics:** ALS overview, progressive motor neuron disease, assistive technology, respiratory care, end-of-life planning
- **Summary:** Covers ALS progression, loss of voluntary muscle function, speech and swallowing difficulties, respiratory support needs, assistive devices and technology, and the intensive caregiving demands including end-of-life decision-making about ventilator support and feeding tubes.

### 1.5 Huntington's Disease

#### Huntington's Disease
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/huntingtons-disease/
- **Topics:** Huntington's disease, genetic testing, movement disorders, psychiatric symptoms, family impact
- **Summary:** Describes the hereditary nature of Huntington's disease, genetic testing considerations, progressive motor/cognitive/psychiatric symptoms, medication management, and the unique family impact given the hereditary nature of the condition.

### 1.6 Multiple Sclerosis

#### Multiple Sclerosis
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/multiple-sclerosis/
- **Topics:** MS types, disease-modifying therapies, relapsing-remitting patterns, caregiver adaptation
- **Summary:** Overview of multiple sclerosis types (relapsing-remitting, secondary progressive, primary progressive), disease-modifying therapies, symptom management, and the variable and unpredictable nature of caregiving needs in MS.

### 1.7 Traumatic Brain Injury

#### Traumatic Brain Injury
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/traumatic-brain-injury/
- **Topics:** TBI severity levels, cognitive and behavioral changes, rehabilitation, family adjustment
- **Summary:** Covers mild to severe TBI, cognitive and personality changes following brain injury, rehabilitation process, and the significant family adjustment needed when a loved one experiences brain injury, including grief for personality changes.

### 1.8 Other Conditions

#### Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD)
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/copd/
- **Topics:** COPD management, oxygen therapy, breathing techniques, activity pacing, exacerbation management
- **Summary:** Covers COPD caregiving including medication management, oxygen therapy, recognizing exacerbations, emergency preparedness, and supporting quality of life with progressive respiratory disease.

#### Diabetes and Caregiving
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/diabetes-caregiving/
- **Topics:** Blood glucose monitoring, insulin management, hypoglycemia recognition, diet and exercise, complications
- **Summary:** Addresses the caregiver role in diabetes management including blood sugar monitoring, medication/insulin administration, recognizing hypo/hyperglycemia, dietary management, and managing complications.

#### Heart Disease
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/heart-disease/
- **Topics:** Heart failure management, cardiac rehabilitation, medication management, lifestyle modifications
- **Summary:** Covers caregiving for heart disease including heart failure symptom management, medication adherence, dietary restrictions (sodium, fluids), activity guidelines, and recognizing warning signs requiring emergency care.

## 2. CAREGIVER SELF-CARE AND WELL-BEING

### 2.1 Caregiver Health and Burnout

#### Taking Care of YOU: Self-Care for Family Caregivers
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/taking-care-you-self-care-family-caregivers/
- **Topics:** Caregiver self-care, stress management, health maintenance, setting boundaries, asking for help
- **Summary:** Foundational resource on caregiver self-care addressing the importance of maintaining physical and emotional health while caregiving. Covers stress recognition, setting realistic goals, asking for and accepting help, maintaining social connections, and the permission to prioritize one's own health.
- **Key subtopics:**
  - Recognizing caregiver stress warning signs
  - Physical health maintenance
  - Emotional well-being strategies
  - Setting boundaries and saying no
  - Building a support network
  - Making time for yourself
  - When to seek professional help

#### Caregiver Depression: A Silent Health Crisis
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/caregiver-depression-silent-health-crisis/
- **Topics:** Caregiver depression, risk factors, symptoms, treatment, intervention
- **Summary:** Addresses the high rates of depression among family caregivers, risk factors (female caregivers, dementia caregivers, co-residing caregivers), symptoms to watch for, barriers to seeking treatment, and available interventions including therapy and medication.

#### Caregiver Burnout
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/caregiver-burnout/
- **Topics:** Burnout symptoms, chronic stress, compassion fatigue, recovery strategies, prevention
- **Summary:** Distinguishes caregiver burnout from normal stress, identifies physical and emotional symptoms (exhaustion, cynicism, reduced efficacy), and provides strategies for prevention and recovery including respite care, support groups, and professional counseling.

#### Emotional Side of Caregiving
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/emotional-side-caregiving/
- **Topics:** Grief, guilt, anger, resentment, ambivalence, emotional complexity of caregiving
- **Summary:** Validates the wide range of emotions caregivers experience, from love and satisfaction to anger, guilt, and grief. Normalizes these feelings and provides strategies for processing complex emotions including anticipatory grief.

#### Grief and Loss
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/grief-and-loss/
- **Topics:** Anticipatory grief, ambiguous loss, bereavement, mourning during caregiving, post-caregiving grief
- **Summary:** Addresses the multiple types of grief caregivers experience, including anticipatory grief during illness progression, ambiguous loss when a loved one's personality changes, and bereavement after death. Covers the transition from caregiver identity.

#### Sleep and Caregivers
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/sleep-and-caregivers/
- **Topics:** Sleep disruption, insomnia, nighttime caregiving, sleep hygiene, fatigue management
- **Summary:** Covers the chronic sleep deprivation many caregivers experience, its health consequences, strategies for improving sleep quality, nighttime caregiving management, and when to seek help for sleep disorders.

### 2.2 Support and Coping

#### Caregiver Support Groups
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/connecting-caregivers/
- **Topics:** Support groups, peer support, online communities, finding support, benefits of caregiver support
- **Summary:** Describes the benefits of caregiver support groups (in-person and online), how to find appropriate groups, what to expect, and how peer connections reduce isolation and provide practical advice.

#### Hiring In-Home Help
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/hiring-home-help/
- **Topics:** Home care aides, hiring process, screening, supervision, costs, agencies vs. independent providers
- **Summary:** Practical guide for hiring in-home caregiving help, covering agency vs. independent hiring, interviewing and screening, setting expectations, legal and tax considerations, costs, and managing the caregiver-employer relationship.

#### Respite Care
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/respite-care/
- **Topics:** Respite services, caregiver breaks, adult day programs, in-home respite, residential respite
- **Summary:** Explains types of respite care (in-home, adult day programs, residential), how to find respite services, funding options, overcoming barriers to using respite (guilt, trust concerns), and the importance of regular breaks for caregiver sustainability.

## 3. PRACTICAL CAREGIVING SKILLS

### 3.1 Daily Care

#### Incontinence
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/incontinence/
- **Topics:** Urinary incontinence types, management strategies, products, dignity preservation, skin care
- **Summary:** Covers types of incontinence (stress, urge, overflow, functional), management strategies, products available, maintaining dignity, skin care to prevent breakdown, and when to seek medical evaluation.

#### Nutrition and Caregiving
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/nutrition/
- **Topics:** Nutrition for care recipients, meal preparation, swallowing difficulties, appetite changes, tube feeding
- **Summary:** Addresses nutritional needs across different conditions, managing appetite changes, swallowing safety (dysphagia), modified texture diets, and the social and emotional aspects of mealtimes.

#### Pain Management
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/pain-management/
- **Topics:** Pain assessment, medication management, non-pharmacological approaches, communicating about pain, palliative approaches
- **Summary:** Covers pain assessment (including in non-verbal patients), medication management (including opioid concerns), non-drug pain management strategies, communicating with healthcare providers about pain, and palliative approaches to pain control.

#### Home Safety
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/home-safety/
- **Topics:** Fall prevention, home modifications, safety assessments, wandering prevention, medication safety
- **Summary:** Room-by-room home safety assessment guide covering fall prevention, bathroom modifications, kitchen safety, fire safety, wandering prevention technology, medication storage, and adaptive equipment.

### 3.2 Legal and Financial Planning

#### Legal Planning for Incapacity
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/legal-planning-incapacity/
- **Topics:** Advance directives, power of attorney, healthcare proxy, living wills, guardianship/conservatorship
- **Summary:** Covers essential legal documents for incapacity planning including durable power of attorney (financial and healthcare), advance directives, living wills, POLST/MOLST forms, and when guardianship/conservatorship may be needed.

#### Financial Issues in Caregiving
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/financial-issues-caregiving/
- **Topics:** Financial planning, benefits programs, insurance, tax considerations, employment impact, paying for care
- **Summary:** Addresses the financial impact of caregiving including lost wages, out-of-pocket costs, navigating insurance and benefits (Medicare, Medicaid, VA), tax deductions, FMLA, and long-term financial planning.

### 3.3 Navigating the Healthcare System

#### Hospital Discharge Planning: A Guide for Families and Caregivers
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/hospital-discharge-planning-guide-families-and-caregivers/
- **Topics:** Discharge planning, care transitions, post-hospital care, medication reconciliation, follow-up care
- **Summary:** Guides families through the hospital discharge process including understanding discharge plans, post-acute care options (skilled nursing, home health, rehabilitation), medication management during transitions, and advocating for adequate support.

#### Working with Your Doctor
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/working-with-your-doctor/
- **Topics:** Healthcare communication, appointment preparation, HIPAA considerations, care coordination, advocacy
- **Summary:** Practical advice on communicating effectively with healthcare providers, preparing for medical appointments, navigating HIPAA privacy rules as a caregiver, coordinating among multiple providers, and advocating for the care recipient.

### 3.4 End-of-Life Care

#### End-of-Life Decision Making
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/end-life-decision-making/
- **Topics:** Hospice care, palliative care, advance care planning, goals of care, comfort care, DNR orders
- **Summary:** Addresses end-of-life decision-making including understanding hospice and palliative care, having goals-of-care conversations, comfort care measures, artificial nutrition and hydration decisions, and supporting the caregiver through this process.

#### Hospice and Palliative Care
- **URL:** https://www.caregiver.org/resource/hospice-and-palliative-care/
- **Topics:** Hospice eligibility, palliative care services, Medicare hospice benefit, comfort focus, bereavement support
- **Summary:** Explains the differences between hospice and palliative care, eligibility criteria, services provided, the Medicare hospice benefit, how to access these services, and the bereavement support available to families.
