## 1. Overview

AARP's Caregiving Resource Center is one of the most comprehensive consumer-facing caregiving hubs in the United States. It serves an estimated 53+ million unpaid family caregivers nationwide and provides free, accessible information across the full spectrum of caregiving needs. AARP positions itself as both an advocacy organization (pushing for policy changes such as the RAISE Family Caregivers Act) and a practical resource provider for day-to-day caregiving challenges.

**Target Audience:** Family caregivers, aging adults, spouses, adult children caring for parents, sandwich-generation caregivers, long-distance caregivers.

### 3.2 AARP Advocacy and Policy Initiatives
- **RAISE Family Caregivers Act:** AARP was a key advocate for this federal legislation (signed 2018) establishing a national caregiving strategy.
- **CARE Act (Caregiver Advise, Record, Enable):** Model state legislation (adopted in 40+ states) requiring hospitals to identify family caregivers, notify them of discharge, and provide instruction on medical tasks.
- **State Caregiver Support Programs:** AARP tracks and advocates for state-level paid family leave, respite care funding, and caregiver tax credits.
- **Paid Family and Medical Leave:** AARP supports federal and state-level legislation for paid leave for caregiving responsibilities.

## 5. Key Recommendations from AARP for Caregivers

1. **Start planning early:** Use the Prepare to Care guide before a crisis occurs.
2. **Build a care team:** Don't try to do everything alone; coordinate with family, friends, and professionals.
3. **Understand legal and financial tools:** Establish power of attorney, advance directives, and financial management plans early.
4. **Prioritize self-care:** Caregiver health deterioration is a major risk; use respite care and maintain personal health.
5. **Leverage technology:** Use apps, telehealth, medication management tools, and smart home devices.
6. **Know your rights:** Understand FMLA protections, state-level caregiver support laws, and the CARE Act.
7. **Connect with community:** Use AARP's online community, local support groups, and the Eldercare Locator.
8. **Track finances:** Document all caregiving expenses for tax purposes and financial planning.
9. **Communicate proactively:** Have regular conversations with care recipients about their preferences and advance directives.
10. **Seek training:** Take advantage of free caregiver training for medical tasks (medication administration, wound care, mobility assistance).
